A mapping is the bridge between one Sonarr series and the SVT Play programme its episodes come from. Sonarr knows a series by its TVDB id; SVT Play knows it by the slug in its URL and knows nothing about TVDB, so nothing links the two and svtplay-arr cannot guess. Until a series is mapped, its episodes are invisible to Sonarr no matter how many are on SVT.

Start with Find mappings below: it walks your Sonarr library and works out the ones it can prove, by comparing episode dates and numbers rather than by matching titles. Everything it is not sure about it lists for you to decide. Or add a single series by hand with Add a series.

This takes a while. One click walks your whole Sonarr library: up to {{ sweep_cap }} unmapped series in a run and at most {{ sweep_request_budget }} requests to SVT, spread out so as not to hammer it. On a large library expect a minute or more with no visible progress, and nothing to read until it finishes. It is safe to leave running — it writes only at the end, and re-running continues where a capped run stopped.

Find mappings searches SVT for every Sonarr series that is not mapped yet — under its own title and Sonarr's alternate titles — and then compares each likely SVT programme's episodes against that series' episodes. It saves only the ones where the episodes themselves agree: same air date, same episode number, on enough episodes that coincidence is not an explanation. Everything else is listed for you to decide, with the count that decided it.
